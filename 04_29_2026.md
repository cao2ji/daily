# Daily Status - April 29, 2026

## Completed

- Fixed `AFIFO_OVERFLOW` and `AFIFO_UNDERFLOW` issues.
  - Root cause: `TRKPLL` can hunt around lock when `w_clk` and `r_clk` are near-coincident.
  - Fix: changed the tolerance from 2 ps to `1.4 ps * 3 + 0.5 ps`.
  - Reason: at the default 200G `wrange`, one TDC step is approximately 1.4 ps. The updated tolerance allows 3 steps plus a 0.5 ps margin for modeled jitter and simulator event ordering before declaring a FIFO error.
